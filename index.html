How to decide Sunday

Do not ask: “Which place is best?”

Ask: which option creates the best total family system — enough ease for Fiona, enough inspiration for Achim, enough joy for the kids, and enough practical safety to actually book.

Decision rule: pick the best cancellable accommodation, not the abstract best destination. A great house can beat a great region.
